# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in laya-top to be safe, respectful, and useful. Be considerate, discuss the work rather than the person, welcome questions asked in good faith, and give specific technical feedback. Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and communication styles.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, sustained disruption, and personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants according to the severity and pattern of behavior. Enforcement decisions should protect affected people, consider context, and avoid exposing reporters unnecessarily.

Report conduct concerns privately using the channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), prefixed with `[conduct]`. Include links or screenshots when safe to do so. Do not use a public issue to report sensitive conduct.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community spaces, and public representation of the project.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
